# Ravenwood connected 3D rooms — 0.11.0

The user-approved pivot replaces turn-based territory-board exploration with
independently loaded, fully 3D wilderness areas. Cities retain their existing
lightweight illustrated presentation. World map → Explore Ravenwood, or the
main menu's Ravenwood Wilds, opens Oldwood Road. The town forest approach's
north-road A interaction also opens it. Its gate action enters Blackthorn's
existing town scenes (hero selection first if required).

Oldwood Road and Briar Crossing load into the same controller, with one scenery
root active at a time. Walking through the north road enters Briar Crossing;
walking back through its south edge returns to Oldwood Road. Return spawns are
inside the area, with a transition guard. The crossing's north trail is explicitly
a future dungeon boundary, not a playable dungeon.

WASD or the existing multitouch joystick moves relative to the camera. Tap the
visible trail to walk along it; manual input cancels the target. Run is a toggle.
The selected approved hero uses speed-matched idle/walk/run. Analytic route
corridors keep the player on the road and bridge, with sampled terrain/deck
heights. This is authored corridor navigation, not general physics traversal or
an open-world navigation mesh. There is no jumping or walking into the stream.

Scenery is original, reproducible Godot mesh construction: textured triangulated
terrain, a curved road, rooted branching trees with alpha-cutout foliage, ferns,
flowers, irregular stones, a cart, palisade, waystone, signs and a timber bridge.
A compatibility-renderer water shader supplies small animated ripples. One
shadowed sun, ambient lighting and distance fog establish the lighting. There
are no paid providers, downloaded asset packs or new native addons. Five small
textures are generated by `tools/environments/make_woodland_textures.py` using
Pillow and NumPy. Their original pixels and procedural source are project-owned.
These sources are independent of the approved hero meshes.

Repeated props use MultiMesh batches instead of thousands of scene nodes. Room
meshes are deterministic from a fixed seed. Old scenery is detached immediately
and freed on the next frame. There are no cinematics, wilderness enemies, combat,
loot, permanent exploration saves or dungeon encounters in this pass. Hero
appearance persistence is unchanged. Desktop compatibility renders and gameplay
tests do not establish Android frame rate; phone performance remains for review.

## Verification

Tests cover both room builds, bounded scene-node counts, hero screen placement,
walkable paths, stream/bridge boundaries, manual cancellation of tap travel,
floor height, two-way transitions, detached old scenery and safe arrival points.
Actual Godot captures review the gate approach, deeper forest and bridge.
